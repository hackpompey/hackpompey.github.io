Everyone

Taking part means agreeing to the Code of Conduct

Our Code of Conduct applies at every Hack Pompey event, in a room in Portsmouth or online, and in our Discord. By taking part, you agree to follow it.

It says what counts as harassment and how photos work at our events. If somebody breaks it, the organisers decide what happens, up to removing them from the event.

November 2026

The online event has its own terms

Hack Pompey Online runs on online.hackpompey.co.uk. That site has its own terms at online.hackpompey.co.uk/terms. If you take part, they apply to you as well as this page and the Code of Conduct. Three rules matter most.

  • You must be 13 or over.
  • You must be a member of the Hack Pompey Discord.
  • If we remove you from the Hack Pompey Discord for breaking the rules, you are removed from the event too.

Something wrong

Reporting a problem

If somebody is breaking the Code of Conduct, or anything about the event makes you uncomfortable, tell us.

  • During the online event, use the report page on the event site.
  • At any time, email contact@hackpompey.co.uk.
  • At an in-person event, talk to any member of the Hack Pompey team.

If somebody is in danger right now, call 999.

Your information

Privacy

What we do with your information is on our privacy page. The online event has its own privacy notice on the event site.

Any questions about this page? Email contact@hackpompey.co.uk, or go back to the Hack Pompey homepage.

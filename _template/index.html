Past events

We’ve been running Hack Pompey events for over 10 years! Some other events we’ve done include Hack the Unexpected, Good Vibes, Sustainability, Hack the Home, Fashion Hack, Wearables, Open Data, and the original Hack Pompey way back in 2014!

If you have any questions at all, please drop us a line at contact@hackpompey.co.uk or on our Discord.

Code of Conduct

We are dedicated to providing a safe, comfortable and harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of their background.

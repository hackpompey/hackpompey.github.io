Sign up on the event site

The whole event runs on its own site, online.hackpompey.co.uk. That is where you make your account, start or join a team, submit what you built and vote. This page is only the introduction.

Sign-up opens there on Friday 23 October at 11am, a month before the event. Make your account before the week starts if you want a vote: accounts made after that can still enter, but not vote.

You will also need to be in the Hack Pompey Discord. Join it now and we will tell you there the moment sign-up opens.

The plan

How the week runs

  1. 01

    Get into the Discord

    The whole thing happens there: the questions, the half-finished screenshots, the going quiet at 2am. You need to be in it whichever way you sign in, and we do check.

  2. 02

    Find people, or do not

    Teams go up to five. Start one, join one, or work alone. Working alone is a normal way to do this and nothing here treats it as second best.

  3. 03

    Build for a week

    Take the theme wherever you want to take it. There is no requirement that the result works properly, only that it exists by the deadline, which is a different and much kinder bar.

  4. 04

    Submit before the deadline

    Code goes on GitHub: a public repository and the commit you are submitting, plus a link people can try, a walkthrough video, or screenshots with a write-up. No code? A walkthrough video instead. Organisers read every entry before anybody else sees it.

  5. 05

    Vote on each other

    Everybody who made an account before the week started gets one vote for People’s Choice, and the organisers pick a Staff Choice. You cannot vote for your own. Results go up when voting closes, and neither prize is worth cheating for.

Questions

FAQs

How good do I need to be?

Not very. People turn up having never finished a project, and people who do this for a living, and they end up on the same team more often than you would think.

How old do I need to be?

Thirteen and up. The whole event runs in a Discord server and Discord accounts have to be 13, so we cannot let anybody younger take part.

Do I need a team?

No. Plenty of people work alone and submit alone. If you want one, the event site lists teams looking for people.

What if I drop out halfway?

Then you drop out halfway. It is a free community event, not a commitment. Tell your team so they are not waiting on you.

Where does my project live?

The code goes on GitHub, in a public repository, so voters can read it. Where it runs is up to you: a link, a video, or screenshots with a write-up.

Is there an in-person one?

Not this time. Every Hack Pompey before this one happened in a room in Portsmouth, and you can see them below.

About us

What is Hack Pompey?

Hack Pompey is a free, social hackathon. For over ten years it has happened in a room in Portsmouth, most often at the University of Portsmouth, and this November is the first one online.

We welcome people from all backgrounds and disciplines to join us, learn something new, and build something together.

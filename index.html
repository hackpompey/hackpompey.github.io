Sign up on the event site

The whole event runs on its own site, online.hackpompey.co.uk. That is where you make your account, start or join a team, submit what you built and eventually vote.

Sign-up opens there on Friday 23 October at 11am, a month before the event. Make your account before the week starts if you want a vote: accounts made after that can still enter, but not vote.

You will also need to be in the Hack Pompey Discord. Join it now and we will tell you the moment sign-up opens.

The plan

How it works

  1. 01

    Get into the Discord

    This is where the community is. You need to be in it for this event. Like, literally. It’s how we’ll keep in touch with eachother throughout the event. Oh, expect plenty of memes and half-baked ideas too.

  2. 02

    Find people, or do not

    Max team size is 5 people. You can start one, join one, or work alone. Working alone is a perfectly fine way to approach a remote hack so don’t fret about not having a team. Come as you are.

  3. 03

    Build for a week

    Take the theme wherever you want to take it. There is no requirement that the result works properly, only that it exists by the deadline, a much lower and kinder bar. The theme is announced at the event start, by the way.

  4. 04

    Submit before the deadline

    Your code will need to be hosted on Github. You’ll submit a link to your public repo and tell us which commit hash we need to look at. In addition, aim to share a link people can try, a walkthrough video, or screenshots with a write-up. No code? A walkthrough video instead. We (the organisers) will read every entry before anybody else sees it.

  5. 05

    Vote on each other’s projects

    Everybody with an account made before the event start gets one vote for the People’s Choice Award, and the Hack Pompey team will pick a Staff Choice. You cannot vote for your own. Results will go up when voting closes, and neither prize is worth cheating for.

Questions

FAQs

How good do I need to be?

Not very. People turn up having never finished a project, and people who do this for a living, and they end up on the same team more often than you would think.

How old do I need to be?

Thirteen and up. The whole event runs in a Discord server and Discord accounts have to be 13, so we cannot let anybody younger take part.

Do I need a team?

No. Plenty of people work alone and submit alone. If you want one, the event site lists teams looking for people.

What if I drop out halfway?

Then you drop out halfway. It is a free community event, not a commitment. Tell your team so they are not waiting on you.

Where does my project live?

The code goes on GitHub, in a public repository, so voters can read it. Where it runs is up to you: a link, a video, or screenshots with a write-up.

Is there an in-person one?

Not this time. Every Hack Pompey before this one happened in a room in Portsmouth, and you can see them below.

About us

What is Hack Pompey?

Hack Pompey is a free, social hackathon. For over ten years it has happened in a room in Portsmouth at the University of Portsmouth, and this November is the first one online.

We welcome people from all backgrounds and disciplines to join us, learn something new, and build something together.
